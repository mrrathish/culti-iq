        What makes this different?

        Image classification alone produces observations. CultiIQ connects those observations to a numerical constraint solver, so the farm plan can change when water availability changes.

        The optimizer checks water feasibility rather than just generating recommendations in natural language.

        Important measurement limits
        A meter's percentage may be a proprietary relative scale, not volumetric moisture. A soil photo cannot measure root-zone moisture. A compound photograph cannot establish exact dimensions or annual sunlight.

        Planning intelligence
        Crop assignment considers manually entered sunlight, illustrative seasonal suitability, most recent crop-family rotation and farm-wide water constraints.
        Crop family rotation is checked using saved history. Record prior crops in Crop Rotation before optimizing.
        Sustainability indicators
        Generate a plan to see measurable planning proxies.
        These are transparent planning indicators, not a validated sustainability score or measured water savings. Soil health and biodiversity require additional data.
        Sunlight model status
        The prototype uses farmer-entered estimated hours. Geometry-derived solar/shadow modeling, north orientation, seasonal shade calculations and photo-based mapping remain future integrations.
